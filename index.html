Blog Framework · TOFU Content Strategy
TOFU–MOFU–BOFU Content Structure

TOFU (Awareness): Broad keyword blogs — "What is French bistro cuisine?" — targeting informational intent, building organic traffic.


MOFU (Consideration): Comparison & guide content — "Best restaurants in Paris 14th arrondissement" — targeting users in research mode.


BOFU (Conversion): Landing pages with strong CTAs — "Book a Table at Bistrotters" — targeting high-intent visitors ready to act.

This framework was applied across multiple client campaigns to improve both organic traffic volume and conversion rates simultaneously.
